[7:21 PM] Touch: The Racket flowering of the Scheme branch of the Lisp family of languages has a great deal of vitality in its community, including its developers.  It has very recently replaced the lowest layers of its implementation with a powerful native machine translation system borrowed from Chez Scheme.  Chez has been nearly as fast as C for some time but was proprietary.  After Chez's author, Kent Dybvig, released Chez as F/LOSS, the Racket developers decided to radically refactor Racket to use Chez's technology.  This sort of refactoring is rarely done because most large and complex software systems are a mess.  It is a testament to the extraordinary quality of Racket's implementation as well as to the energy of some key developers that this was possible and has now happened.   Chez Scheme based Racket is already faster than any previous Racket and should continue to become more efficient as the optimization opportunities of the new implementation are exploited.  Even more important is the confirmation that everything Racket is of such high quality!
For those who would like to explore Racket, either as your first Lisp or as your latest Lisp adventure, I highly, highly recommend that you do the https://docs.racket-lang.org/quick/ tutorial.  If you would like assistance in getting started, I would be happy to provide it.  I would also love to follow up with anyone in our Limitless community who has done this tutorial to discuss what knowing Racket and related computing technology makes possible and what it might make available for you!
