* Why Use Orgmode Markup

As of 2025, [[https://orgmode.org/][Org Mode]] is the best [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markup_language][Document Markup Language]] which
- is [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_file][Plain Text]]
  - readable by humans without any fancy software
  - editable with any simple [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_editor][Text Editor]]
- is Concise and Pleasant To Read
- supports *extremely powerful features*
  - beyond any other markup language!
- has all features optional, thus supporting
  - Unstructured text
  - Structured text
  - Any mixture of the two!

** FAQ

*** Does OrgMode require [[https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/][Gnu Emacs]]

Not for simple editing
- Use any Text Editor to work with Org Mode
- Including online editors at [[https://github.com/][GitHub]], [[https://about.gitlab.com/][GitLab]], et al.

Not for simple OrgMode Power Features
- Several editors plugins to support some advanced OrgMode features

Yes for *all the OrgMode Power Features*
- So consider learning Emacs and OrgMode together!
- try our guide to [[https://github.com/GregDavidson/computing-magic/blob/main/Software-Tools/Emacs/emacs-readme.org][Learning Emacs and OrgMode]]

*** How Can I Learn OrgMode

Try our guide to [[https://github.com/GregDavidson/computing-magic/blob/main/Software-Tools/Emacs/emacs-readme.org][Learning Emacs and OrgMode]]

Otherwise, you'll find lots of tutorials at
- [[https://orgmode.org/worg][Worg: the Org-Mode Community Site]]

*** Why Don't You Use ...?

Note: OrgMode documents can be automatically converted into these
other formats!

- [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markdown][Markdown]] :: inconsistent dialects, less powerful
  - The CommonMark "standard" is not always followed in practice.
  - Lacks the kind of powerful features that OrgMode has
- [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML][XML]] :: super general, more complete syntax yet less powerful
  - Verbose
  - Hard to Read, Hard to Edit
  - Lacks the kind of powerful features that OrgMode has
